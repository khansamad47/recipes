# Chicken Pulao
[Link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xfrH0fKpQ_E)

## Ingrediants
* Basmati Rice Soaked 2.75 cup
* Chicken 600g with bone
* 3 Onions sliced (1 for garnish)
* 1/4 Yogurt cup
* Garam masala (long, 2 big elachi, 4 small elachi, 1 medium cinamon stick, zeera 1/2 tablespoon, black pepper 10)
* 1/2 cup oil
* 1 tomato
* 1 tbsp bombay biryani masala
* 3-4 green chillis
* 6 Allo Bugara
* 1/2 lemon
* Hara Daniya / Powdni 
* 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
* Chicken cube 1/2 cube
* 1 tbsp salt
* red chilli powder, daniya powder

## Steps
1. Fry onions until light golden, take some out for garnish (brown thoses)
2. Add chicken
